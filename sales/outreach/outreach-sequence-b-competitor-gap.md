# Outreach Sequence B: Competitor Gap Analysis

> **Purpose:** 4-email outreach sequence that leads with competitive intelligence — showing the prospect how their competitors are out-messaging them and how to close the gap.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad for cold outreach to DTC brands in competitive niches where competitor comparison is compelling

## Contents

### Sequence Overview
- **Email 1 (Day 1):** "I compared your [emails/ads/landing page] to [Competitor] and noticed something"
- **Email 2 (Day 4):** Specific competitive insight with a screenshot or example
- **Email 3 (Day 8):** How we'd close the gap — preview of what better content looks like for their brand
- **Email 4 (Day 14):** Breakup email with a deliverable attached (mini teardown or one-page audit)

### Per-Email Structure
- Subject line (3 options per email)
- Body copy (75-125 words max)
- CTA
- Personalization points
- Internal notes

### Research Guide
- How to identify competitors (SimilarWeb, Facebook Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency)
- What to compare: email signup flows, welcome sequences, product pages, ad creative
- How to frame the gap without being negative about the prospect's current work

### When to Use
- Best for: brands in crowded niches (skincare, supplements, fashion, food) where competitors are visibly outperforming them in content
- Lead source: industry rankings, Shopify top stores, social media brand discovery

### Success Metrics
- Target open rate: 50%+ (curiosity-driven subject lines perform well)
- Target response rate: 10-15%
- Target meeting-to-proposal rate: 60%+
