# Outreach Sequence A: Free Audit Offer

> **Purpose:** 4-email outreach sequence that leads with a free content audit of the prospect's existing emails, landing pages, or ads. The audit demonstrates our expertise and creates a natural bridge to a paid engagement.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad for cold outreach to DTC brands that have visible content to audit

## Contents

### Sequence Overview
- **Email 1 (Day 1):** Personalized observation about their brand + offer to audit one specific thing
- **Email 2 (Day 4):** Quick value-add — share one specific, actionable insight about their content
- **Email 3 (Day 8):** Social proof — brief case study or result from a similar brand
- **Email 4 (Day 14):** Breakup email — friendly close with open door

### Per-Email Structure
- Subject line (3 options per email)
- Body copy (75-125 words max — short and punchy)
- CTA (one clear ask per email)
- Personalization points (what to customize per prospect)
- Internal notes (purpose of this email, what response to hope for)

### Personalization Guide
- Where to find audit-worthy content on their site
- What to look for (weak subject lines, generic product descriptions, missing email flows)
- How to reference specific products or campaigns they're running

### When to Use
- Best for: brands doing $1M-$10M revenue with existing email/content programs that have clear room for improvement
- Lead source: Shopify store directories, Facebook Ad Library, competitor customer lists

### Success Metrics
- Target open rate: 45%+
- Target response rate: 8-12%
- Target audit-to-proposal rate: 50%+
