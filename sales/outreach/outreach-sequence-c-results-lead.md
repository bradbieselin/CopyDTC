# Outreach Sequence C: Results-Led

> **Purpose:** 4-email outreach sequence that leads with specific results CopyDTC has achieved for similar brands. Best used once we have 2-3 solid case studies.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad for outreach to brands where we can draw a direct line between their situation and a past client's results

## Contents

### Sequence Overview
- **Email 1 (Day 1):** Lead with a specific result for a similar brand — "We helped [similar brand type] increase [metric] by X%"
- **Email 2 (Day 4):** Expand on the case study — what we did and why it worked
- **Email 3 (Day 8):** "Here's what we'd do for [their brand]" — specific, personalized recommendations
- **Email 4 (Day 14):** Breakup email with a useful resource attached (relevant blog post, teardown, or template)

### Per-Email Structure
- Subject line (3 options per email)
- Body copy (75-125 words max)
- CTA
- Personalization points
- Internal notes

### Case Study Matching Guide
- How to match prospects to relevant case studies
- Similarity criteria: same niche, same revenue range, same challenge, same content type
- How to present results without revealing confidential client information

### When to Use
- Best for: brands that are actively investing in content but not seeing results
- Requires: at least one relevant case study or documented result
- Lead source: brands running ads, brands with active email programs, brands with content teams

### Success Metrics
- Target open rate: 40%+
- Target response rate: 12-18% (highest of all sequences — results get attention)
- Target close rate from responses: 25%+
