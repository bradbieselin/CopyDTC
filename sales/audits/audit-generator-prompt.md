# Audit Generator Prompt

> **Purpose:** Claude prompt that generates a professional content audit for a prospect's brand. The audit analyzes their existing content across channels and provides specific, actionable recommendations — serving as both a value-add and a sales tool.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad when a prospect responds to outreach and we want to demonstrate expertise before sending a proposal

## Contents

### The Prompt
- Input: prospect's website URL
- Input: prospect's email signup (to receive their welcome sequence)
- Input: prospect's Instagram, TikTok, Twitter handles
- Input: any ads found in Facebook Ad Library or Google Ads Transparency
- Input: prospect's top 3 competitors

### Output Sections
1. **Executive Summary** — 3-4 sentences on the overall state of their content and the biggest opportunity
2. **Channel-by-Channel Audit**
   - Website copy (homepage, product pages, about page)
   - Email marketing (welcome sequence, promotional emails if visible)
   - Social media (Instagram, TikTok, Twitter)
   - Paid ads (Meta, Google if visible)
   - Blog/content marketing
3. **Per-Channel Assessment**
   - What's working (be genuine — find real positives)
   - What's not working (be specific and constructive)
   - Quick win recommendation (one thing they could fix today)
   - Strategic recommendation (what we'd do if we managed this channel)
4. **Competitive Comparison** — how their content stacks up vs competitors
5. **Priority Recommendations** — top 5 actions ranked by impact, with estimated results
6. **How CopyDTC Would Help** — brief, non-salesy bridge to our services

### Quality Bar
- Audit must be genuinely useful even if the prospect never hires us
- Specific examples from their actual content (not generic advice)
- Compliments should be real, critiques should be constructive
- Total length: 3-5 pages
