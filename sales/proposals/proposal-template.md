# Proposal Template

> **Purpose:** Professional proposal template for pitching CopyDTC services to prospects. Combines audit findings, strategic recommendations, scope of work, pricing, and terms into a compelling document.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad after an audit or discovery call when a prospect is ready to discuss engagement

## Contents

### Proposal Structure
1. **Cover Page** — CopyDTC + client brand name, "Content Partnership Proposal," date
2. **Executive Summary** — 3-4 sentences: their challenge, our solution, expected results
3. **Understanding Your Brand** — show we did our homework (brand snapshot, audience, goals)
4. **The Opportunity** — what their content could be doing for them (based on audit findings)
5. **Our Approach** — how CopyDTC works (AI-powered + human expertise, Brand Voice Bible, template system)
6. **Scope of Work** — specific deliverables by month, organized by content type
7. **Timeline** — pilot month day-by-day, then ongoing monthly cadence
8. **Investment** — pricing by tier with what's included in each
9. **Why CopyDTC** — 3-4 differentiators (speed, quality, DTC specialization, AI-powered consistency)
10. **Case Studies / Social Proof** — relevant results from similar brands
11. **Next Steps** — clear path from "yes" to first delivery (sign agreement → complete questionnaire → kickoff call)
12. **Terms** — brief summary of key terms (full agreement to follow)

### Customization Points
- Tailor scope of work to what the audit revealed they need most
- Lead with the content type that will show results fastest
- Price recommendation based on their revenue and content needs
- Timeline adjusted for any urgent campaigns or launches

### Design
- Clean, branded PDF
- Include their logo alongside ours
- Visual scope of work (content calendar preview)
- Pricing presented as investment with ROI framing
