# Pricing Guide

> **Purpose:** Internal pricing reference for CopyDTC services. Covers tier structure, deliverable counts, add-on pricing, and guidelines for custom quotes.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad when creating proposals and discussing pricing with prospects

## Contents

### Tier Structure

#### Starter — $3,000/month
- Best for: brands doing $500K-$2M revenue, just getting started with professional content
- Includes: Brand Voice Bible, 4 email sequences, 4 product descriptions, 8 social media posts, monthly content calendar
- Deliverables: ~15-20 pieces/month
- Communication: bi-weekly check-in, email support

#### Growth — $5,000/month
- Best for: brands doing $2M-$5M revenue, ready to scale their content engine
- Includes: everything in Starter + landing pages, blog posts, ad copy, SMS
- Deliverables: ~25-35 pieces/month
- Communication: weekly check-in, Slack access

#### Scale — $10,000/month
- Best for: brands doing $5M+ revenue, need a full content partner
- Includes: everything in Growth + strategy, advanced analytics, priority delivery, dedicated account manager
- Deliverables: ~50-70 pieces/month
- Communication: multiple weekly touchpoints, priority response

### Add-On Pricing
- Additional email sequence: $500
- Landing page (product): $750
- Blog post (SEO): $600
- Ad copy set (one platform): $400
- Social media week (7 posts): $350
- Full website copy: $3,000-$5,000 (custom quote)

### Custom Quote Guidelines
- When to quote custom: enterprise clients, unusual deliverable mixes, project-based work
- How to calculate: base tier + add-ons, minimum viable scope for their goals
- Discount policy: annual commitment = 10% discount, referral clients = first month 15% off

### Pricing Psychology
- Always present pricing as investment, not cost
- Lead with the tier most likely to close, then show the tier above as aspirational
- Never discount without removing scope — protect the value of our work
- Pilot month is always full price (sets the expectation)
