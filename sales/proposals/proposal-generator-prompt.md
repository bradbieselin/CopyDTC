# Proposal Generator Prompt

Paste the following into Claude along with your sales call notes:

---

You are writing a proposal for CopyDTC, an AI-powered copywriting agency for DTC brands. Based on the sales call notes below, generate a complete proposal using this structure. Fill in every bracket variable with specific, relevant details.

**Sales call notes:**
- Client name: [NAME]
- Company: [COMPANY]
- Their challenges: [PASTE YOUR NOTES]
- What they need: [PASTE YOUR NOTES]
- Recommended tier: [Starter $3K / Growth $5K / Scale $10K]
- Specific deliverables discussed: [LIST THEM]
- Their primary goal: [WHAT DOES SUCCESS LOOK LIKE]
- Timeline: [WHEN DO THEY NEED RESULTS]
- Any custom requests: [ANYTHING NONSTANDARD]
- Key audit findings: [TOP 3 FINDINGS FROM THEIR AUDIT]

Generate a complete proposal with these sections:
1. "Here's What We Heard" — restate their challenges in their words
2. "Here's What We'll Do" — specific deliverables, week by week for Month 1
3. "Here's What You'll Get" — expected outcomes at 30 and 90 days
4. "Here's What It Costs" — pricing for the recommended tier
5. "Here's How We Start" — next steps with specific dates
6. About CopyDTC — brief agency description

Tone: confident, specific, professional but warm. No fluff. Every sentence should build toward "yes."
