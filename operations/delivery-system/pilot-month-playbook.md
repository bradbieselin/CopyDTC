# Pilot Month Playbook

> **Purpose:** Step-by-step guide for executing a flawless first month with a new client, ensuring they see enough value to convert to an ongoing retainer.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad (and future account managers) during the first 30 days of every new client engagement

## Contents

### Week 1: Foundation & Quick Wins
- Days 1-2: Onboarding completion (questionnaire, Brand Voice Bible)
- Days 3-5: First batch of deliverables — prioritize highest-impact content
- Quick win strategy: identify the one piece of content that will show results fastest
- Communication cadence: daily check-ins via email or Slack during week 1

### Week 2: Full Production
- Content calendar execution begins
- Batch delivery schedule (2-3 deliverables per batch)
- Mid-week check-in call
- Feedback loop established

### Week 3: Optimization & Expansion
- Incorporate feedback from weeks 1-2
- Begin tracking content performance metrics
- Introduce additional content types based on client needs
- Proactive strategy suggestions

### Week 4: Review & Retention
- Compile monthly performance report
- Prepare month-end review presentation
- Draft Month 2 content calendar
- Retention conversation strategy

### Overdelivery Moments
- When to surprise clients with bonus content
- How to identify opportunities for unexpected value
- Template for "thought of you" proactive suggestions

### Red Flags to Watch For
- Client going silent (no feedback responses)
- Excessive revision requests (voice mismatch signal)
- Scope creep indicators
- How to address each red flag
