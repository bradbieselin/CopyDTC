# Email Templates

> **Purpose:** Every common internal and client-facing email template CopyDTC needs. Copy, customize, and send. Covers the full client lifecycle from first outreach to offboarding.
> **Status:** Scaffold — full content to be built in subsequent prompts
> **Used by:** Brad (and future team members) for all standard client communications

## Contents

### Sales Emails
1. **Cold outreach — initial contact** — first touch email for prospects
2. **Cold outreach — follow-up 1** — 3 days after initial, value-add angle
3. **Cold outreach — follow-up 2** — 7 days after initial, social proof angle
4. **Cold outreach — breakup** — final follow-up, door-open close
5. **Audit delivery email** — sending a free content audit to a prospect
6. **Proposal delivery email** — sending a formal proposal

### Onboarding Emails
7. **Welcome email** — immediately after contract signed, sets expectations
8. **Questionnaire delivery email** — sending the brand questionnaire
9. **Kickoff call confirmation** — calendar invite follow-up with agenda
10. **Kickoff recap email** — post-call summary with action items
11. **Brand Voice Bible delivery** — sending the completed Brand Voice Bible for approval
12. **Content calendar delivery** — sending Month 1 content plan

### Delivery Emails
13. **First batch delivery** — sending first deliverables with strategic context
14. **Subsequent batch delivery** — standard delivery email
15. **Revision delivery** — sending revised deliverables after feedback
16. **Requesting feedback** — asking client to review deliverables (with deadline)
17. **Feedback follow-up** — gentle nudge when feedback is overdue

### Reporting & Reviews
18. **Monthly report delivery** — sending the monthly performance report
19. **Review call scheduling** — booking the month-end review call
20. **Review call recap** — post-review summary with next month plan

### Retention & Growth
21. **Month 2 kickoff** — transitioning from pilot to ongoing
22. **Upsell suggestion** — proposing a tier upgrade based on results
23. **Testimonial request** — asking for a testimonial when results are strong
24. **Referral ask** — requesting referrals when relationship is strong
25. **Quarterly check-in** — proactive check-in for long-term clients

### Offboarding
26. **Offboarding acknowledgment** — professional response to cancellation
27. **Final deliverables** — sending remaining content and Brand Voice Bible
28. **Thank you and door-open** — graceful goodbye with re-engagement incentive

### Administrative
29. **Invoice reminder** — friendly payment reminder
30. **Access request** — asking for analytics/platform access
31. **Holiday/OOO notice** — out of office or holiday schedule communication
